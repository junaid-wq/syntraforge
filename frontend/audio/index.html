About AudioTrim

AudioTrim provides a visual waveform editor directly in your browser so podcasters, musicians, students, and video editors can trim audio clips, remove silence, and create custom ringtones in seconds.

How to Use AudioTrim in 3 Steps

  1. Load any local MP3, WAV, M4A, or OGG audio file into the waveform timeline above.
  2. Drag the start and end handles to isolate the exact segment you want to keep.
  3. Apply optional fade-in/fade-out transitions and export your trimmed audio file.

Technical Specifications & Supported Formats

Supported Audio FormatsMP3, WAV (PCM), M4A / AAC, and OGG Vorbis
Editing PrecisionInteractive zoomable waveform with millisecond start/end timestamps
Audio TransitionsSmooth fade-in and fade-out envelopes to prevent click artifacts
Privacy Architecture100% client-side Web Audio API decoding — private recordings stay local

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a file size or duration limit?

Because AudioTrim uses your device's own memory rather than uploading to a server, you can trim long lectures, podcasts, and music tracks without upload caps.

Are my voice memos or unreleased tracks uploaded anywhere?

No. All waveform rendering and audio slicing take place locally inside your browser tab.
