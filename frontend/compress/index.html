💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images saved on a server?

Never. Images stay completely in local RAM and are discarded when you close or reload the browser tab.

Is there any file size or quantity limit?

No arbitrary limits. You can compress as many high-resolution photos as your device memory can handle.
