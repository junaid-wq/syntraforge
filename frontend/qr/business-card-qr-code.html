How to Make a Print-Ready QR Code for a Business Card

A business card QR code links physical networking directly to your portfolio, LinkedIn profile, or digital vCard contact file. Because SyntraForge encodes your destination straight into a static QR matrix in your browser, your printed cards never expire or break behind a paid subscription wall.

1. Pick a Clean, Short Destination

Paste your portfolio URL, Linktree, or vCard details above. Shorter URLs produce simpler QR grids with larger modules that scan faster on small business cards.

2. Size at Least 20 mm x 20 mm

Keep your printed QR code at least 0.8 inches (2 cm) wide with a clean white quiet-zone border around all four sides so phone cameras lock focus instantly.

3. Export SVG or High-DPI PNG

Download SVG vector format for Canva, Illustrator, or commercial offset printing, and test-scan your proof on both iOS and Android before printing a batch.

Business Card QR Code FAQs

What is the minimum size for a QR code on a business card?

Print your QR code at least 20 mm x 20 mm (0.8 x 0.8 inches) with a clear margin around the edges. Smaller prints can blur on textured or matte cardstock.

Will my business card QR code ever expire after printing?

No. SyntraForge generates direct static QR codes with no middleman redirect server, so your code works forever as long as your destination link stays active.

Should I export SVG or PNG for business card printing?

Use SVG when designing in Adobe Illustrator, Figma, or Canva for print shops so every edge stays razor-sharp at 300+ DPI. PNG works great for digital cards and quick mockups.

What colors scan best on matte or dark business cards?

Always use a dark foreground on a bright background (such as black or deep navy on white). Avoid inverted light-on-dark codes or glossy foil finishes that reflect camera flash.

What should I link a business card QR code to?

Most professionals link to a mobile-friendly portfolio, LinkedIn profile, booking page, or downloadable vCard contact card so recipients can save their details in one tap.
