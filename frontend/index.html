📖 How to Extract Video Frames Losslessly

  1. Load Video: Drag any local MP4, WebM, or MOV file into the dropzone.
  2. Scrub & Seek: Use native playback or arrow keys to cue the exact millisecond.
  3. Choose Format: Select Lossless PNG for pixel-perfect stills, or JPEG/WebP for lighter files.
  4. Export: Click "Capture Frame" to save the native dimensions directly to your device.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are my videos ever sent to a server?

No. Processing is 100% sandboxed within your local browser runtime. Zero bytes are uploaded.

What is the maximum resolution supported?

FrameGrab supports full 4K UHD (3840×2160 landscape or 2160×3840 vertical 9:16) and 60FPS video.
