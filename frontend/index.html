How to Extract Video Frames Losslessly

  1. Load Media: Drag any MP4, WebM, or MOV file into the upload zone or click to select from your device.
  2. Pinpoint Frame: Play the clip and use the scrubber or arrow keys (◀ / ▶) to step to the precise millisecond.
  3. Select Format: Choose lossless PNG for graphics and thumbnails or 98% quality JPEG for smaller file sizes.
  4. Export: Click "Grab Frame" to save the high-resolution still image directly to your local storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my video files stored on a server?

No. SyntraForge FrameGrab executes entirely in your client-side browser runtime. Zero bytes are uploaded over the internet.

What is the maximum supported resolution?

SyntraForge supports up to full 4K UHD (3840x2160) and high-framerate 60FPS video, limited only by your browser's decoding capability.
