Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a JPG to 50KB without losing quality?

Set the target size to 50KB in the tool and let it adjust JPG quality automatically. It lowers quality in small steps until the file fits, then stops. For photos, this keeps visible detail intact. If the result looks soft, resize the image width first, then compress again.

Is it safe to compress JPG files online with SyntraForge?

Yes. SyntraForge runs the compression in your browser using JavaScript. Your image is never uploaded to a server, so no one else can see, store, or copy it. You can verify this by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads; the tool still works.

Why do government forms and job portals ask for a 50KB JPG?

Many portals cap upload size to save storage and speed up processing. Exam forms, visa applications, and job sites commonly set limits such as 20KB, 50KB, or 100KB for photos and signatures. Check the portal's rules for the exact size, dimensions, and format before you upload.

Can I compress a JPG to exactly 50KB?

The tool aims for just under 50KB, so the file stays within the limit. It cannot hit an exact byte count because JPG quality changes the size in small jumps. That works for portals that require under 50KB or a 50KB maximum. For a strict minimum size, check the portal's rules first.

No. SyntraForge adds no watermark, asks for no email, and has no sign-up wall. The tool is free forever. You can compress as many files as you like. Because processing happens on your device, large images depend on your device's memory, not a server quota.
