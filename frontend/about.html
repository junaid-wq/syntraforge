About SyntraForge

SyntraForge is an independent suite of browser-native media and productivity utilities built for creators, designers, students, and web developers. Our goal is to replace slow, ad-cluttered upload portals with instant tools that run directly inside your web browser.

Why Local Browser Execution Matters

Traditional online converters require uploading gigabytes of personal videos, photos, or voice recordings to remote servers—wasting bandwidth and exposing private files. Every utility on SyntraForge leverages modern browser APIs (HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and hardware-accelerated video decoding) so your files stay strictly on your own device from start to finish.

Our Core Tool Suite

  • FrameGrab (frame.syntraforge.me): Extract uncompressed 4K still frames from MP4, MOV, and WebM videos with frame-by-frame precision.
  • Image Studio (image.syntraforge.me): Compress and convert JPG, PNG, and WebP images to target KB sizes without server uploads.
  • QR Studio (qr.syntraforge.me): Generate permanent, non-expiring static QR codes in PNG and SVG formats for URLs, WiFi, UPI, and vCards.
  • AudioTrim (audio.syntraforge.me): Crop and trim MP3, WAV, M4A, and OGG audio tracks with visual waveform precision.
